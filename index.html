About

I started in life sciences — a B.Sc in Zoology & Biotechnology, then a diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology — before spending close to four years as a medical coder across healthcare organizations. That work meant reviewing large volumes of patient data daily, staying compliant with DHA, HIPAA, and CMS standards, and coordinating with physicians, billing teams, and insurers to keep records accurate and claims moving.

That combination — comfort with detail, regulatory discipline, and working across teams — is what I now bring to data analytics. Since 2025 I've been building a structured path through SQL, Power BI, and Tableau, with a hands-on project at the end of each stage, collected below.
